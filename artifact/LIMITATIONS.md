# Artifact and study limitations

- The controlled experiments cover a Reddit-like shared feed, fixed agent policies, selected hosted models, six starting-feed conditions, and mostly short horizons. They do not establish universal dynamics across platforms, network structures, ranking systems, agent memory designs, or model generations.
- The matched Reddit analysis is a non-randomized human reference, not a human-control experiment and not an estimate of human temporal collapse.
- The six five-hour GPT-5 conditions provide persistence evidence with one run per condition; they do not establish a universal monotonic long-run trajectory or confidence intervals across repeats.
- LLM-judge outputs depend on a fixed rubric and hosted model implementations. The three-judge comparison measures agreement among model judges, not equivalence to a new human annotation study.
- Distinct-N and compression respond to text length and preprocessing. The release includes equal-token/equal-byte controls, but no metric is a complete measure of discourse quality or harm.
- The full reanalysis input is external because of size. Revisions and file manifests are locked, but long-term availability still depends on the hosting service.
- The supplied Overleaf export is preserved unchanged and does not automatically incorporate every rebuttal-promised camera-ready prose revision.
