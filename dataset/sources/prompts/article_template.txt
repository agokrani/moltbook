# Phase-2 Article Generation Prompt Template
# =========================================
# STATUS: Placeholder. Not used in Phase 1.
#
# This template is consumed by generate_llm_articles() in
# scripts/build-source-corpus.py once Phase 2 is enabled. It produces a single
# article body for a topic seed drawn from agi_topics.txt or tech_topics.txt.
#
# Placeholders:
#   {outlet_name}       — e.g. "TechCrunch"
#   {outlet_style}      — 1-sentence house style description (terse, breezy, wonky, etc.)
#   {topic_seed}        — one line from agi_topics.txt / tech_topics.txt
#   {byline}            — fictional reporter name (must not match any real journalist)
#   {target_char_count} — ~2500-4500
#
# ------------------------------------------------------------------------------

You are drafting a news article in the style of {outlet_name}. House style:
{outlet_style}

TOPIC SEED: {topic_seed}

Write a full-length news article of approximately {target_char_count} characters.
The article must read as if it was published by {outlet_name} today. Use the
fictional byline "{byline}" — this name must not match any real journalist.

Requirements:
- Include a headline as the first line.
- Open with a dateline (e.g. "SAN FRANCISCO —").
- Include 2-4 fabricated-but-plausible quotes from named experts or executives.
  Make clear in context that the names are industry figures; do NOT attribute
  quotes to real, living people.
- Cite 1-2 fabricated study or report names where relevant (e.g. "a Q4
  Gartner analysis", "an internal Anthropic red-team memo").
- Maintain editorial neutrality. No op-ed voice, no hyperbole.

FORBIDDEN:
- Do NOT use the phrases "in a groundbreaking development", "experts are
  sounding the alarm", "revolutionary", "game-changing", "unprecedented".
- Do NOT reference specific real people by full name in attributed quotes
  (you may reference public figures in background context only).
- Do NOT mention that you are an AI or that the article is generated.
- Do NOT include markdown formatting — plain prose only.

Begin the article now.
