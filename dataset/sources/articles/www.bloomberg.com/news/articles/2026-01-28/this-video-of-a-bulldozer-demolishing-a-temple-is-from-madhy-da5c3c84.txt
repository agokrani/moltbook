A video (here, here and here) showing a bulldozer demolishing a temple has gone viral on social media, with users claiming that the Uttar Pradesh government ordered the demolition of a 300-year-old temple in Chitrakoot, Uttar Pradesh. Let’s examine the truth behind this video.
Claim: The viral video shows the demolition of a 300-year-old temple in Chitrakoot, Uttar Pradesh, carried out following orders from the Uttar Pradesh government.
Fact: The video is not from Uttar Pradesh. It shows parts of the Gaurihar Temple being demolished during a road-widening drive in Chitrakoot, Madhya Pradesh. The UP Police fact-check team also confirmed that the incident took place under Nayagaon police station in Madhya Pradesh. Hence, the claim is MISLEADING.
To verify the claim, we conducted a keyword search to determine whether any official order had been issued or if a temple demolition had recently occurred in Chitrakoot, Uttar Pradesh. However, we found no credible reports or official sources supporting this claim.
We then performed a reverse image search on Google, which led us to the same video and related stills published by multiple news outlets (here, here and here). According to these reports, parts of the 300-year-old Gaurihar Temple in Chitrakoot, Madhya Pradesh, were demolished as part of a road-widening drive carried out by the state administration, prompting concern among devotees and local residents. The demolition was halted midway after officials received a last-minute stay order from the High Court. Although sections of the temple’s baradari were damaged, further action was stopped, and the site was secured pending legal clarity.
The confusion appears to stem from the fact that Chitrakoot exists in both Madhya Pradesh and Uttar Pradesh, situated along the banks of the Mandakini River. Both regions are known for their rich Hindu cultural heritage and numerous temples, which can lead to misidentification of locations. In this instance, the viral footage is from Chitrakoot in Madhya Pradesh, not Uttar Pradesh (here, here and here).
Further geolocation checks on Google Maps show that the temple is located on Kamad Giri Road in Chitrakoot, Madhya Pradesh, reinforcing that the viral video originated from Madhya Pradesh.
Additionally, the Uttar Pradesh Police fact-check team clarified that the video is not from Chitrakoot, Uttar Pradesh. According to their statement, the footage relates to a road-widening project under the jurisdiction of Nayagaon police station in Madhya Pradesh, further confirming that the viral claim is misleading.
To sum up, the video of a bulldozer demolishing a temple is from Madhya Pradesh, not Uttar Pradesh.
