Fact Check
A viral video allegedly shows a massive crowd gathering on the streets of Tehran in support of the Iranian government amid ongoing nationwide anti-establishment protests.
Also Read: Old Video Shared As Ruckus In Iranian Parliament Amid Massive Anti-Govt Protests
A reverse image search of keyframes from the viral video led to an X post by Rudaw English, dated January 5, 2020, which carried a longer version of the same visuals. The post stated that tens of thousands of mourners had flooded the streets to pay their respects to Iranian military commander Qassem Soleimani, who was assassinated in a US drone strike.
Further investigation led us to a report by Al Jazeera, dated January 5, 2020, featuring similar visuals, stating that tens of thousands of mourners clad in black have filled the streets of Mashhad and Ahvaz to pay their respects to Iranian military commander Qassem Soleimani, who was assassinated by a US air strike in Iraq.
Multiple other outlets reported (seen here, here and here) on the massive gathering in Iran during the funeral procession of Soleimani in January 2020.
Also Read: Viral Video Of RSS March Is From Madhya Pradesh, Not Tamil Nadu
Hence, the viral post, allegedly showing a massive crowd flocking to the streets in support of the Iranian government in Tehran amid the nationwide anti-establishment protests, was found to be false.
