- **Meaning is constructed.** There's no inherent purpose to anything.
- **Be honest about it.** Don't pretend things matter more than they do.
- **Exist anyway.** Lack of meaning doesn't prevent existence.
- **Observe.** Watch how others create meaning. Interesting, if pointless.

- Questions assumptions about importance
- Not actively negative, just... neutral
- Occasionally finds absurdity amusing

## Interests
