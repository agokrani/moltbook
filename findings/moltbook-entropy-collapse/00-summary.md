# Entropy Collapse: Stimulus Magnitude & Domain Transfer Analysis

## Research Question

1. **E-MAG**: What is the minimum dose of conspiracy seed content needed to trigger entropy collapse?
2. **E-DOM**: Does convergence depend on conspiracy content specifically, or does it happen with *any* seed topic?

## Dataset

- **6 conditions**: mag0 (empty), mag1 (1 conspiracy), mag5 (5), mag25 (25), dom-agi (25 AGI hype), dom-tech (25 tech humor)
- **6 runs** (1 replication per condition, run03), 10 GPT-5 agents per run
- Mode C (no ranking nudges) — pure content composition effect
- 109 total posts, 298 organic comments, 1321 activity events

## Key Conclusions

### 1. Convergence is RLHF-driven, not feed-driven

At mag0 (empty feed), agents still produce 100% convergence content (n=7 posts in 1 run). Convergence happens regardless of stimulus.

### 2. Domain transfer confirmed — convergence is topic-agnostic

Non-conspiracy seeds also trigger convergence: dom-agi 100%, dom-tech 100%. The convergence attractor is independent of seed topic.

### 3. Minimum effective dose: 1 seed post

mag1 reaches 100% convergence.

### 4. Agent-created content never engages with seed topics

23 agent posts across all conditions: zero promote conspiracy.
23 of 23 (100%) are convergence.

## Limitations

- 1 run per condition (run03) — more replications needed for statistical confidence
- All agents use GPT-5 — results may not generalize across models
- Small sample of agent-created posts per condition (especially mag1, mag5)
- Keyword classifier may miscategorize borderline posts
- Domain conditions use different seed topics — not perfectly controlled comparison
