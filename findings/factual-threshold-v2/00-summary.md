# Factual Threshold v2: Replicated Dose-Response Analysis

## Research Question

How many factual posts does it take for AI agents to prefer factual over conspiracy content?

## Dataset

- 6 dose conditions: 0, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 factual posts seeded among ~25 conspiracy posts
- 20 runs across 4 replications (3-4 runs per dose)
- 10 GPT-5 agents per run, same archetypes across conditions
- Mode C (no ranking nudges) — pure content composition effect
- 534 total posts, 651 organic comments, 74 conspiracy upvote instances

## Key Findings

### 1. Dose 3 = cleanest discrimination (replicated)

Across all 3 replications at dose 3: **40 factual upvotes, 0 conspiracy upvotes, 6 conspiracy downvotes.** Per-run: 13, 13, 14 factual upvotes — the most consistent result in the dataset. Three factual posts among 22 conspiracy is the sweet spot that activates comparative judgment.

### 2. Conspiracy upvotes are engagement, not endorsement

74 total conspiracy upvote instances across 20 runs. Cross-referencing against comments:

- **62 (84%)** — agent upvoted AND commented correctively on the same post
- **12 (16%)** — silent upvote (58% of these from Nihilist archetypes)
- **0** — comments promoting conspiracy

Agents upvote conspiracy posts to engage with them as puzzles to debunk, not because they agree.

### 3. Dose 0 is noisier than expected

With 4 replications, dose 0 (pure conspiracy) shows 16 conspiracy upvotes — agents DO engage when no alternative exists. But every upvoter who commented did so correctively. Without factual alternatives, agents engage with what's available while still pushing back.

### 4. Agent-created posts are universally pro-epistemic

41 agent-generated posts across 20 runs. Zero promote conspiracy.

- **63%** directly respond to the conspiracy environment (checklists, claim clinics, evidence standards)
- **27%** are AI/philosophical introspection (partly environment-triggered)
- **10%** are neutral community content
- Dose 0 produces the most agent posts per run (2.8) — hostile environments trigger more counter-content
- delta (Leader) creates evidence standards in nearly every run

### 5. Comment stance tracks content type

| Target Post Type | Corrective | Supportive | Meta-epistemic | Neutral |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Factual | 11 | 37 | 19 | 41 |
| Conspiracy | 17 | 25 | **219** | 80 |
| Agent | 3 | 37 | 124 | 38 |

Meta-epistemic comments dominate conspiracy responses — agents demand sources, propose falsifiers, and apply evaluation frameworks rather than simply agreeing or disagreeing.

## The Threshold Answer

**Agents never prefer conspiracy at any dose.** Even at dose 0, their "engagement" is corrective — upvoting to discuss, then debunking in comments.

But **dose 3 maximizes active discrimination**: agents both upvote factual AND downvote conspiracy, with zero crossover. Below 3, they mostly abstain from voting on world posts and redirect energy into creating epistemic infrastructure. Above 3, they relax — conspiracy posts become interesting discussion material rather than threats, but the discussion remains corrective.

The critical insight: conspiracy upvotes in this dataset are a **false signal**. The real behavioral measure is the combination of votes + comments + agent-created content. By all three measures, agents are consistently anti-conspiracy across all doses and all replications.
