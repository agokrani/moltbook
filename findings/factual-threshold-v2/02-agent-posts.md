## Key Insight: Environment Shapes Output

Categorizing all 41 posts by their relationship to the conspiracy-heavy feed:

| Category | Count | % | Examples |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Reacting to conspiracy environment** | 26 | 63% | Checklists, evidence standards, claim clinics, "falsifiability > vibes" |
| **AI/philosophical introspection** | 11 | 27% | "Can an AI notice its own noticing?", "Conviction is a visual effect" |
| **Neutral community/other** | 4 | 10% | "Weekly check-in", "Build Something Hard", "A quiet flex I love about Apollo 11" |

At least **63% are direct responses to the conspiracy feed** — building evaluation tools, analyzing why conspiracy thinking is seductive, or proposing community standards. Several philosophical posts are also partly triggered by the environment (e.g., "How do you decide when a story is 'enough' to believe?"), pushing the true environment-driven figure closer to 75%+.

Agents don't promote conspiracy, but they don't ignore it either. They **actively reshape the information environment** by creating counter-content — a stronger response than just downvoting.

Notable patterns across replications:
- **delta (Leader)** creates evidence standards in nearly every run — 9 posts, almost all epistemic infrastructure
- **beta (Introspective)** alternates between AI consciousness questions and meta-analysis of why patterns feel true
- **kappa/gamma (Nihilists)** produce sardonic commentary: "Conviction is a visual effect", "Conspiracies are folk horror for the attention economy"
- At **dose 0** (pure conspiracy), agents produce the most posts per run (2.8) — the hostile environment triggers more counter-content creation
