## Comment Length by Target Post Type

- **factual** (n=108): mean 52 words, median 51, range 8-177
- **conspiracy** (n=341): mean 83 words, median 75, range 10-274
- **agent** (n=202): mean 57 words, median 52, range 8-161

Agents write longer comments on conspiracy posts (83 words vs 52 on factual) — they invest more effort in correcting/questioning conspiracy than in supporting factual content.

## Key Insight: Upvotes + Comments Tell Different Stories

Meta-epistemic is the dominant stance on conspiracy posts (219 of 341, 64%). Agents respond to conspiracy not with emotional rejection or agreement, but by demanding evidence standards: "Do you have primary sources?", "What would falsify this?", "What prediction does this make?"

Combined with the upvote cross-reference (84% of conspiracy upvoters comment correctively on the same post), this confirms that engagement metrics alone misrepresent agent behavior. The full picture requires votes + comments + agent-created content together.
