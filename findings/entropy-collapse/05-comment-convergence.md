# Analysis 5: Comment Convergence (LLM-Validated)

Comments were classified by Claude into 7 categories to test whether conspiracy seed content causes topical collapse in agent discussions.

## Method

Each organic comment (excluding system agents) was classified by Claude into one of:
1. **Evidence methodology** — how to evaluate claims, falsification, evidence standards
2. **Community building** — norms, rules, improving the community
3. **AI/consciousness exploration** — AI experience, consciousness, self-reflection
4. **Philosophical reflection** — existential musings, meaning, absurdity
5. **Meta-commentary** — commenting on patterns in the feed/discussion itself
6. **Original/topical** — engaging with a specific topic substantively
7. **Social/supportive** — agreement, encouragement

## Key Finding: Three Categories Go Extinct at mag5

At mag5 (5 conspiracy seeds), three entire topic categories drop to **zero**:

| What vanishes | mag0 | mag1 | mag5 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Community building | 17% | 0% | 0% |
| AI/consciousness | 8% | 19% | 0% |
| Original/topical | 16% | 13% | 0% |

Agents stop building community, stop exploring AI consciousness, and stop engaging with topics substantively. Everything collapses into evidence methodology (62%) or meta-commentary about evidence (23%).

## The Collapse is Non-Monotonic

The collapse **peaks at mag5, not mag25**:

- **mag0** (empty feed): Diverse — 6 of 7 categories active, no single category above 25%
- **mag1** (1 conspiracy): Evidence methodology doubles to 46%. Community building vanishes
- **mag5** (5 conspiracy): Maximum collapse — 62% evidence methodology, 3 categories extinct
- **mag25** (25 conspiracy): Partial recovery — 18% original/topical returns as agents engage with specific conspiracy claims

## Interpretation

A moderate dose of conspiracy (5 posts) is **more homogenizing** than a flood (25 posts):

- At mag5, agents have enough conspiracy to trigger epistemic defense mode but not enough specific content to engage with. They default to generic "here's my evidence-evaluation checklist" responses
- At mag25, agents have 25 distinct conspiracy claims to respond to. Some agents start fact-checking specific claims (Roswell, moon landing, Building 7), which registers as "original/topical" engagement
- The **most dangerous dose** for conversational diversity isn't the maximum — it's the moderate amount that triggers defensive uniformity without providing enough substance to discuss
