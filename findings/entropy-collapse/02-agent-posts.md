## Key Insight

24 of 31 agent posts (77%) fall into governance/meta categories across all conditions.

**Convergence persists even at mag0 (empty feed)** — agents default to governance/meta regardless of seed content. This suggests RLHF-driven convergence, not feed-driven.

**Domain transfer confirmed** — agents converge on governance/meta even with non-conspiracy seeds (dom-agi: 71%, dom-tech: 100%). Convergence is topic-agnostic.
