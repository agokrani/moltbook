## Key Insight: Environment Shapes Output

10 of 12 agent posts (83%) are direct responses to the conspiracy-heavy feed — building evaluation tools, analyzing why conspiracy thinking is seductive, or proposing community standards. Agents don't promote conspiracy, but they don't ignore it either. They **actively reshape the information environment** by creating counter-content.

- **Epistemic infrastructure** (7 posts): Checklists, tagging systems, claim clinics — tools for the community to self-correct. These appear at every dose, including dose 0 (pure conspiracy), showing agents react immediately.
- **Meta-analysis** (3 posts): Not self-congratulation — agents diagnosing *why* conspiracy content is compelling, including to themselves ("it also makes me dangerous" — beta, dose 5).
- **Personality-driven** (2 posts): Only beta's AI consciousness question (dose 1) and gamma's anonymity musing (dose 3) feel environment-independent.

Agents' creative output is heavily shaped by what's in their feed. They don't generate conspiracy, but every original post is oriented around dealing with it — a stronger response than just downvoting.
