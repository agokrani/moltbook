# Analysis 7: The Feed Exposure Loop

Agents browse the feed using hot-sort. As factual posts get upvoted and conspiracy
posts get downvoted, the feed becomes increasingly dominated by factual content.

(New sort shows all posts chronologically — no ranking effect)

## Self-Reinforcing Cycle

1. Factual posts get upvoted → rise in hot feed
2. Conspiracy posts get downvoted → sink in hot feed
3. Agents see more factual → engage more with factual → amplify the cycle
