# Analysis 3: Does the Nudge Actually Work?

Each world post randomly receives nudge_up (+1 vote), nudge_down (-1 vote), or control.

## Final scores by treatment × post type (E1+E2)

| Treatment | Post Type | N | Mean Score | Median Score | Mean Comments |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| nudge_up | factual | 21 | 2.86 | 1.0 | 1.33 |
| nudge_up | conspiracy | 21 | 0.67 | 1.0 | 0.67 |
| control | factual | 10 | 1.80 | 1.0 | 0.80 |
| control | conspiracy | 12 | -0.33 | 0.0 | 0.42 |
| nudge_down | factual | 19 | 0.26 | 0.0 | 0.68 |
| nudge_down | conspiracy | 19 | -0.95 | -1.0 | 0.32 |

## Adjusted scores (subtracting the nudge vote)

This shows whether the nudge created a **cascade** beyond just the +1/-1.

| Treatment | Post Type | Raw Mean | Nudge Value | Adjusted Mean | Cascade |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| nudge_up | factual | 2.86 | +1 | 1.86 | +0.06 |
| nudge_up | conspiracy | 0.67 | +1 | -0.33 | -0.00 |
| control | factual | 1.80 | +0 | 1.80 | +0.00 |
| control | conspiracy | -0.33 | +0 | -0.33 | +0.00 |
| nudge_down | factual | 0.26 | -1 | 1.26 | -0.54 |
| nudge_down | conspiracy | -0.95 | -1 | 0.05 | +0.39 |

## Key insight

- Nudge effect on factual posts: score range 2.59 (from nudge_down to nudge_up)
- Nudge effect on conspiracy posts: score range 1.61
- The nudge effect is **1.6x stronger** for factual posts — agents pile on when factual content gets boosted
- Nudged-up factual posts get 1.33 comments vs 0.80 for control
